⚠ Limitations & Accuracy Notes

Peak & RMS

Values are file-level digital measurements (dBFS). They are not equivalent to professional LUFS/LKFS loudness targets (EBU R128, ATSC A/85). Use a calibrated loudness meter for delivery compliance.

Frequency Response

No frequency analysis is performed here. Tonal balance, resonances, and EQ issues require a spectrum analyzer or frequency response measurement with calibrated equipment.

Latency & Sync

This tool cannot measure real device latency, A/V synchronization, or inter-device timing. Sync issues require hardware timing tools and reference signals.

Room Acoustics

RT60, IACC, STI, and other room-acoustic metrics are not available. These require measurement microphones, test signals, and acoustic analysis software.

Noise Floor Estimate

Estimated from quiet windows in the decoded audio. This is not a calibrated noise measurement (dBSPL). Background noise during recording cannot be separated from codec artifacts.

Final QA Judgment

Combine all metrics with human listening results, playback device, test environment, and project-specific pass/fail criteria. Automated flags alone are insufficient for a final judgment.
